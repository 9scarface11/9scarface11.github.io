02 // An Engineer

Engineering Across the Entire Stack

Modern robotics requires more than just isolated software algorithms or mechanical design. My work lives at the exact intersection where hardware design meets deterministic control and data-driven perception.

End-to-End Execution: From CAD prototypes and embedded C++ drivers up to high-level ROS 2 nodes and PyTorch models.

Simulation to Real World: Validating motion plans in Gazebo and RViz2 before hardware deployment.

Data-Driven Prognostics: Time-series deep learning for predictive maintenance and real-time defect classification.
